## Overview

Digital Odyssey was created to accompany a physical exhibition exploring the history of digital art. The website serves as a virtual gallery, allowing users to interact with key pieces and learn about the artists.

### Key Features

- **Virtual Gallery**: Navigate through 3D spaces to view artworks.
- **Interactive Timeline**: A scroll-based journey through art history.
- **Artist Spotlights**: Deep dives into the lives and techniques of pioneers.

### Design Approach

The site itself is an art piece. We used experimental navigation patterns and generative visuals to reflect the innovative nature of the subject matter.
