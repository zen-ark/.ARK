## Overview

Urban Flow simplifies the daily commute by integrating multiple transit modes into a single, cohesive experience. From subway schedules to bike-sharing availability, users can plan their journeys with ease.

### Key Features

- **Multi-modal Routing**: Combine bus, train, and bike share in one trip.
- **Live Updates**: Real-time notifications for delays and service changes.
- **Offline Maps**: access critical navigation information without data.

### Design Approach

We focused on legibility and speed. The interface is clean and uncluttered, prioritizing the information users need when they are on the go.
